## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an authoritative, institutional platform for civil justice intelligence and legal informatics. Built on a foundation of unyielding precision, it projects supreme judicial gravitas, impartiality, and unassailable clarity. The aesthetic merges editorial legal tradition with the rigor of modern civic-scale infrastructure.

The core visual style is an elevated, sharp-edged editorial minimalism. Surfaces are stark, clinical, and architectural. Instead of rounded, consumer-facing software tropes, the system utilizes disciplined right angles, razor-thin structured grid dividers, and uncompromising typographic hierarchy. The user experience evokes the solemnity and reliability of legal briefs, high-court records, and institutional registries—engineered without frivolous ornament to maximize trust, reading endurance, and decisive action under critical conditions.

## Colors

The color palette is engineered for WCAG 2.1 AAA legibility and sovereign authority. High-contrast tonal relationships reinforce informational hierarchy without optical fatigue.

- **Primary (`#1E1B4B` - Midnight Indigo):** The institutional anchor. Used for primary interactive actions, high-level structural framing, executive badges, and authoritative indicators.
- **Secondary (`#4338CA` - Deep Judicial Blue):** Interactive states, active tab highlights, link accents, and secondary actions requiring distinct optical focus.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7` - Procedural Sky):** Informational callouts, active statutory cross-references, and procedural progress metadata.
- **Neutral Primary (`#0F172A` - Slate Black):** The primary reading ink for running copy, statutory texts, and dense evidentiary tables.
- **Neutral Muted (`#475569` - Slate Subtitle):** Secondary structural labels, metadata headers, and contextual descriptions.
- **Neutral Tertiary (`#64748B` - Slate Detail):** Inactive icons, breadcrumb dividers, timestamp details, and auxiliary codes.
- **Canvas & Surfaces:** Primary workspace backdrop is crisp `#F8FAFC`, stepping into pure `#FFFFFF` for analytical cards and docket containers, with `#F1F5F9` providing contrast for static gutters and toolbars.
- **Functional / Severity Tokens:** Strict clinical signaling:
  - Critical / Denial: `#DC2626` over `#FEF2F2` (stroke: `#FCA5A5`)
  - Warning / Prerequisite: `#D97706` over `#FFFBEB` (stroke: `#FCD34D`)
  - Validated / Qualified: `#16A34A` over `#F0FDF4` (stroke: `#86EFAC`)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the editorial prestige and authoritative heritage of **Newsreader** with the functional clarity and institutional precision of **Public Sans**.

- **Newsreader (Headlines & Document Titles):** Imparts solemnity and editorial polish. Used for case titles, section headers, legal brief summaries, and macro-level findings. It commands respect, elevates complex legal doctrines, and anchors content pages with literary dignity.
- **Public Sans (Body, Forms & Structural Labels):** Designed for institutional and civic utility. Offers uncompromising clarity at dense scales across evidentiary tables, input labels, metadata matrices, and body copy. It prevents visual crowding during long analytical sessions.
- **Implementation Rules:**
  - Case numbers, docket timestamps, and statutory provisions use `label-md` or `label-sm` with tabular lining figures.
  - Section titles set in Newsreader must not drop below 18px (`headline-sm`) to preserve serif integrity.
  - Subheadings, metadata pairings, and table cell entries always utilize Public Sans.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is disciplined, modular, and content-first, driven by a 12-column structural grid with consistent rhythmic intervals.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, minimum 32px (`2rem`) margins. Content caps at a maximum width of 1440px for analytical clarity.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 24px (`1.5rem`) margins. Docket cards reflow into 2-column or full-width analytical views.
  - **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) safe outer margins. Multi-column forms collapse into single vertical tracks.
- **Spacing Rhythms:**
  - `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) govern tight internal groupings: form labels to inputs, badge padding, statutory citation tags.
  - `space-md` (16px) is the baseline component interior padding and standard stacking gap.
  - `space-lg` (24px) establishes separation between distinct procedural stages, card sections, and table groupings.
  - `space-xl` (40px) orchestrates major document partitions and primary dashboard canvas blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids amorphous, decorative blurs in favor of architectural stratification, structural keylines, and controlled low-opacity ambient drop shadows.

- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC`. The foundational backdrop where analytical work takes place.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Containers & Sheets):** Pure `#FFFFFF` anchored by a crisp, continuous 1px border (`#E2E8F0`). Enhanced with a sharp, low-offset shadow: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Tier 2 (Elevated Panels & Dropdowns):** `#FFFFFF` paired with border `#CBD5E1` and an elevated shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Critical Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` framed by a prominent `#0F172A` structural rule or deep shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`. Backdrops use `#0F172A` at 60% opacity with zero blur to keep text sharply readable through dismissal layers.

## Shapes

The design system adheres to an absolute **0px border radius (Sharp)** across every surface, input, badge, modal, and interactive component. 

This strict geometric discipline evokes classical legal printing, official parchment, and institutional certainty. Right angles eliminate decorative levity, transforming interface components into architectural records and structural data fields. Every button, dossier panel, and data card presents clean, unyielding edges that line up flush against the 12-column layout grid.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#1E1B4B` background, `#FFFFFF` text (`Public Sans`, 600 weight), 0px radius, 1px solid `#1E1B4B`. Padding: 10px 20px. Hover: `#4338CA`. Active: `#0F172A`. Focused: 2px offset ring in `#4338CA`.
- **Secondary:** `#FFFFFF` surface, `#0F172A` text, 1px solid `#CBD5E1`. Hover: `#F1F5F9` background with `#0F172A` border.
- **Destructive:** `#DC2626` background, `#FFFFFF` text, 1px solid `#DC2626`. Hover: `#B91C1C`.
- **Tertiary / Docket Action:** Transparent background, `#4338CA` text, underline on hover, 0px borders.

### Input Fields & Selects
- **Base Style:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, 0px radius, padding 10px 14px. Text is `#0F172A` in `Public Sans`. Placeholder text: `#64748B`.
- **Focus State:** 1px solid `#1E1B4B`, outward ring of 1px solid `#1E1B4B`. Never rounded.
- **Error State:** 1px solid `#DC2626` with red explanatory label positioned 4px beneath the bottom border.
- **Labels:** Uppercase `label-sm` (`Public Sans`, 700 weight, 11px), tracking +0.05em in `#475569`, positioned 6px above the field.

### Cards & Dossier Panels
- **Structure:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) surface, 0px radius, bounded by 1px solid `#E2E8F0`. 
- **Header Accents:** High-tier legal dossiers incorporate an authoritative top hairline: 2px solid `#1E1B4B`.
- **Inner Padding:** 24px (`space-lg`) on desktop; 16px (`space-md`) on mobile.

### Chips & Badges
- **Statutory / Status Badges:** 0px radius, uppercase `label-sm`, padding 3px 8px, 1px solid border.
  - *Verified / Safe:* `#F0FDF4` bg, `#16A34A` text, `#86EFAC` border.
  - *Pending / Caution:* `#FFFBEB` bg, `#D97706` text, `#FCD34D` border.
  - *Disqualified / Alert:* `#FEF2F2` bg, `#DC2626` text, `#FCA5A5` border.
  - *Statutory Citation:* `#F1F5F9` bg, `#1E1B4B` text, `#CBD5E1` border.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Shape:** Checkboxes are 16x16px squares (0px radius); Radios are 16x16px diamond-cut squares rotated 45 degrees or sharp native squares with an internal dot.
- **Border:** 1.5px solid `#475569`.
- **Checked State:** `#1E1B4B` fill with `#FFFFFF` crisp geometric check mark icon.

### Lists & Docket Tables
- **Table Headers:** `#F1F5F9` background, 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, typography `label-sm` in `#475569`.
- **Rows:** `#FFFFFF` background, bottom border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`. Hover state triggers `#F8FAFC`. Zero alternating striped backgrounds; clarity is maintained through line rules.
- **Data Density:** Cell padding is strictly 12px 16px. Numerical codes and statutes align right with tabular figures.

### Special Component: Statutory Quotation Callout
- **Structure:** `#F8FAFC` background, bordered exclusively on the left by a 4px vertical bar of `#1E1B4B`. Remaining edges feature 1px solid `#E2E8F0`.
- **Content:** Newsreader italic body text (`body-lg`) accompanied by a Public Sans bold statutory reference citation below.
